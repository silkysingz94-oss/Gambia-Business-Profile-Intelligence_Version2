Data & methodology

Important limitation: This is an independent planning and audit dashboard. It does not connect to Google Business Profile, does not scrape Google Maps, and cannot confirm that a business listing exists or that a link works. Checks must be completed manually by the business owner or authorised auditor. A production integration would require approved Google APIs, OAuth consent and appropriate permissions.

Suggested routine: review profiles monthly, after a change of telephone number, website, opening hours, ownership or service provider, and before major tourism or promotional periods.
